## Derived normal bridge table — verify module mapping before use

Assumptions needing physical confirmation: non-inverting input buffer, RPWM/LPWM reach opposing IN inputs, EN reaches both INH inputs, correct motor terminals. “Forward” is a wiring convention, not a chip property.

| Both EN | RPWM | LPWM | Expected normal bridge action |
|---|---|---|---|
| LOW | any | any | Both outputs disabled; coast |
| HIGH | 0 | 0 | Both low-side switches active; brake |
| HIGH | PWM | 0 | Drive one polarity during PWM HIGH; low-side brake during LOW |
| HIGH | 0 | PWM | Drive opposite polarity during PWM HIGH; low-side brake during LOW |
| HIGH | 1 | 1 | Both high-side switches active; brake |

**DESIGN IMPACT:** do not close D-013 just because a 3.3 V command seems to work at 5 V buffer supply. Bench success does not guarantee HC noise margin across devices, supply and temperature. Conversely, the chip threshold alone does not certify the whole module at VCC=3.3 V. Identify the buffer, actual VCC circuit, pull-ups and loaded output limits; obtain the human's recorded supply decision.

## QTR acquisition conflict — no semantics changed

Repository references: AGENTS R4; BEHAVIOR B4.1 and B16; HARDWARE 8; P0 0.4. A 10+1500 us blocking timeout path exceeds both the 1000 us period and the 800 us execution budget, before any other work. Four sensors in parallel do not shorten the longest timeout.

Validated upstream capability: [Pololu library tag 4.0.0 QTRSensors.cpp](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pololu/qtr-sensors-arduino/4.0.0/QTRSensors.cpp), `readPrivate`, lines 524–576, uses a 10 us delay and polling until the configured maximum. It is not an asynchronous solution. [Zephyr GPIO documentation](https://docs.zephyrproject.org/latest/hardware/peripherals/gpio.html) supports edge callbacks generically; this does not establish available UNO Q EXTI channels, timestamp accuracy, installed-core support, or interrupt cost.

Options requiring approval and evidence:

1. **Threshold-oriented acquisition:** finish a fresh binary white/not-white decision shortly after the largest calibrated threshold, while representing raw values above that threshold as censored, not fabricated 1500 us measurements. This changes B4.1 raw measurement semantics; needs approved threshold budget, calibration path, equality/noise handling and disconnected-sensor behavior. Current 300 us defaults are not measured justification.
2. **Asynchronous full RC measurement:** charge/release, capture falling edges and timeout later, publish generation/time/validity. Full 1500 us observations cannot be restarted every 1000 us on the same pins without truncation; a slower acquisition cadence or another explicit change is unavoidable. Core ticks must distinguish fresh samples from repeats. Approval must define maximum sample age, response deadline and stale handling.
3. **Change hardware or timing requirements:** only under explicit human decision; no timeout reduction, pin reassignment, new part, or slower loop is authorized by this report.

Recommendation for decision preparation: evaluate option 1 against actual white/black distributions first, and option 2 if full raw timing is essential. This recommendation is not approval to implement either. Required regressions after approval: threshold-1/equal/+1; disconnected/high timeout; simultaneous four edges; charge/release skew; rollover; duplicate generation must not advance confirmation; stale frame behavior; persistent white; all-white; noise transitions; and complete worst-case on-board tick measurement with timeout/interrupt load. A desktop benchmark cannot establish R4.

## Bench evidence needed for all unknowns

- QTR: identify exact PCB, scope OUT with sensor isolated from the UNO Q during both power sequences and supply transients; capture maxima/minima. Obtain approved interface/supply choice before connecting. Then measure four sensors over actual black/white/start-line surfaces at installed height, timing distribution, noise and disconnected timeout. A DMM alone can miss startup spikes.
- JS200XF: identify bought revision/photos/manual; measure detection polarity and OUT unloaded plus known safe loads with MCU disconnected; obtain stage/drive-limit confirmation or characterize sufficient interface margins. Confirm approved range method and actual black-target range.
- MZ80: identify wire variant; measure unconnected OUT and response to a known safe load/pull-up with MCU disconnected, both detect states, sensor power off/on. Establish internal pull-up and leakage; approve divider/pull-up wiring from those results.
- IBT-2: inspect component marks and unpowered continuity; establish any EN pull-up and boot/off behavior. Confirm loaded logic levels at actual VCC before driver operation. Output polarity/brake tests belong to authorized P2 B4; any motor-capable flash or run requires fresh STAND OK/RING OK for that run.
- Titan/JS5230: record actual part markings and measurements; performance quantities remain bench/ring evidence, not vendor guarantees.

Nothing here constitutes PINMAP OK, a motor authorization, or a phase gate.
